# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical facts directly.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private data.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner using the contact method on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
